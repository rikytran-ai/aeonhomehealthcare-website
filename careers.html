Careers

Do work that actually matters to someone.

We hire caregivers who treat clients the way they would treat their own parents. If that sounds like you, we would like to meet you.

Why caregivers stay with us.

Aeon is a small, family-owned agency, and that changes what the job feels like. You are matched with clients carefully and kept with them, so you build real relationships instead of bouncing between strangers. The owner answers the phone, knows your name, and works with your schedule.

Personal Care Attendant (PCA)

Assist clients with bathing, grooming, dressing, mobility, meal preparation, and medication reminders. Experience is helpful but not required; we train the right people.

Companion / Homemaker

Provide companionship, light housekeeping, laundry, and grocery shopping. Ideal for caring, reliable people who want meaningful part-time work.

What we look for

  • 18 years or older with reliable transportation
  • Able to pass a criminal background check and Employee Misconduct Registry check
  • Legally authorized to work in the United States
  • Patient, dependable, and comfortable working in clients' homes
  • Bonus: Vietnamese, Spanish, or Chinese language skills

Flexible schedules: part time, full time, weekends, or overnight. Clients across Houston, Sugar Land, Katy, Missouri City, and nearby areas.

To apply: call (713) 240-2518 and ask about caregiver openings, or send a message through the contact form mentioning careers.
