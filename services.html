Our services

In-home care that fits real life.

From a few hours of help each week to full-time live-in support, we build a care plan around what your family actually needs.

01 / Personal Care

Personal Care Assistance

Hands-on help with the activities of daily living. Done with patience, dignity, and the kind of attention that comes from really knowing someone.

Bathing & Grooming

Safe assistance with showers, baths, hair care, shaving, oral care, and nail care.

Dressing

Help selecting clothing and getting dressed in ways that feel comfortable and familiar.

Toileting & Hygiene

Discreet, respectful assistance with restroom needs and continence care.

Mobility & Transferring

Safe movement from bed to chair, walker assistance, and fall prevention.

Meal Preparation

Nutritious meals planned around dietary needs, preferences, and what is in the fridge.

Medication Reminders

On-time reminders and assistance with prescribed routines (non-medical, non-administering).

02 / Companion & Homemaker

Companion & Homemaker

The non-medical side of staying at home. Someone to talk to, a tidy kitchen, errands run, and the household kept up.

Companionship

Conversation, reading together, board games, a walk outside. The small things that fight isolation.

Light Housekeeping

Dishes, dusting, vacuuming, bed making, and tidying. Not a cleaning service, but enough to keep things livable.

Laundry

Wash, fold, and put away clothing and linens, including delicates.

Grocery Shopping

Shopping with the client or for them, working from a list and budget.

Respite Care

Coverage for family caregivers. A few hours, an overnight, or a long weekend so you can rest.

Medicaid accepted

We work with three Medicaid programs.

Aeon Home Health Care is a Medicaid-contracted agency. We do not bill Medicare, private insurance, or accept private pay. If you are enrolled in one of the plans below, your services are likely fully covered.

Medicaid Managed Care
Molina Healthcare

STAR+PLUS members get personal care, companion, and homemaker services through our network contract.

Medicaid Managed Care
Community Health Choice

Houston-based nonprofit Medicaid plan. We are a contracted in-home support provider.

Texas Medicaid Waiver
DADS

Texas Department of Aging and Disability Services programs, now administered through HHSC.

Not sure which plan you have? Bring your Medicaid card to a free consultation and we will verify eligibility on the spot.

Free consultation

Tell us about your situation.

We will listen, ask a few questions, and let you know honestly whether we are the right fit. If we are not, we will point you somewhere good.
