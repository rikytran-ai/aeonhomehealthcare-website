Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. This page explains what information Aeon Home Health Care collects when you call, visit this site, or send us a message, and what we do with it.

The short version

We collect only what we need to respond to your inquiry and coordinate care. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. The sections below cover the details.

What we collect

When you use the contact form on this site, we collect the information you enter: your name, phone number, email address, your relationship to the person needing care, the type of help you are looking for, your Medicaid plan if you know it, and anything you write in the message field.

When you call our office, we collect what you tell us over the phone in order to schedule a consultation or coordinate services.

Like most websites, our hosting and security providers automatically log basic technical information from every visit, such as IP address, browser type, and pages viewed. We use this only for security and site performance, not to build a profile of individual visitors.

Why we collect it

We use the information you provide to respond to your inquiry, schedule a free consultation, verify Medicaid eligibility with the relevant managed care program (Molina Healthcare, Community Health Choice, or a DADS/HHSC waiver program), and coordinate the caregiving services you request.

Who sees it

Your information is seen by Aeon Home Health Care staff directly involved in responding to your inquiry and coordinating your care. We share the minimum necessary information with your Medicaid managed care organization when that is required to verify coverage or authorize services. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

How long we keep it

We keep client and inquiry information for as long as needed to provide services and to meet the record-keeping requirements that apply to Texas home care agencies and Medicaid-contracted providers. If you contact us and do not become a client, we keep your inquiry only long enough to follow up, then remove it from active use.

How we protect it

This site is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and we apply reasonable technical safeguards to protect information submitted through our contact form. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps appropriate to the sensitivity of the information involved.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete inquiry information that is not tied to an active care record. Email care@aeonhomehealth.com or call (713) 240-2518 and we will respond within a few business days.

Children

Our services are intended for adults arranging care for themselves or a family member. This site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and revise the date at the top. Significant changes will be noted here.

Questions
