Our story

Built by a family, for other families.

Aeon Home Health Care started in 2017 in southwest Houston with a simple idea: keep care personal, keep it consistent, and never grow so big that the people on our payroll forget the people in their care.

A small agency, by design.

Loan Pham founded Aeon after years of watching families in her community struggle to find help they could trust. The big franchise agencies sent a rotating cast of strangers. The cheap independents lacked oversight. There was a real gap in the middle.

So she built one. Texas licensed. Background-checked caregivers. Real schedules with familiar faces. The kind of agency where the owner answers her own phone and knows every client by name.

That is still how we run today. We stay small on purpose because care quality lives or dies on whether the caregiver actually knows the person they are caring for.

What guides us

Three things we will not compromise on.

Dignity first

The person we care for is the customer, not the family member writing the check. Their preferences, their pace, their household rules. We adapt to them.

Consistency

Same caregiver, same schedule, whenever we can make it work. People with cognitive changes especially need familiar faces. We protect that hard.

Honest conversation

If we are not the right fit, we will tell you. If something is not working in a care plan, we will say so. Pretending fixes nothing.
