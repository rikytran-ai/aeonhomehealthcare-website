What we do

Practical, hands-on support built around your loved one.

Our caregivers focus on the everyday details that keep someone safe, comfortable, and engaged at home. No two care plans look the same.

Personal Care Assistance

Help with the everyday activities that matter most for dignity and independence.

  • Bathing and grooming
  • Dressing
  • Toileting and hygiene
  • Mobility and transferring
  • Meal preparation

Companion and Homemaker

Non-medical support that keeps daily life running and the household well cared for.

  • Companionship and conversation
  • Light housekeeping
  • Laundry
  • Grocery shopping

Respite Care

Family caregivers need rest too. We step in for a few hours or a few days so you can recharge.

  • Hourly, daily, or weekend coverage
  • Continuity with familiar caregivers
  • Care plans that mirror your routine
  • Medication reminders
  • Safety and fall prevention

Medicaid accepted

We are a Medicaid-only agency.

Aeon Home Health Care contracts directly with three Texas Medicaid programs. If you are enrolled in one of these, your home care services are likely covered at no out-of-pocket cost.

Medicaid Managed Care
Molina Healthcare

STAR+PLUS members get personal care, companion, and homemaker services through our network contract.

Medicaid Managed Care
Community Health Choice

Houston-based nonprofit Medicaid plan. We are a contracted in-home support provider.

Texas Medicaid Waiver
DADS

Texas Department of Aging and Disability Services programs, now administered through HHSC.

Not sure which plan you have? Bring your Medicaid card to a free consultation and we will verify eligibility on the spot.
